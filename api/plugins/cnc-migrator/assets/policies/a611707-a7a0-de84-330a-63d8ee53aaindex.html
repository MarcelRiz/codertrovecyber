Purpose of this Policy

This policy defines acceptable usage, practices and behaviour for staff and relevant third parties when using company-issued IT assets. It intends to let the staff know of any restrictions that are placed by the management.

Policy Statements

Unacceptable Behaviours

Sample & Co. characterises the following behaviours as unethical and therefore unacceptable:

  • Any activity which purposely seeks to gain unauthorised access to any resources,
  • Disrupts the intended use of the resources,
  • Wastes resources (people, capacity, computer) through such actions,
  • Destroys the integrity of computer-based information, and/or
  • Compromises the privacy of other users.

Your Responsibilities

As an employee, contractor or associate of the company, you have the following responsibilities when using company-issued IT assets and resources:

  • You must read, understand and always comply with all information security policies.
  • You must consult your reporting manager, or the information security team to clarify any doubts pertaining to these policies.
  • You must report the following to your line management or to information security team:
    • Suspected or actual security incidents,
    • Security weaknesses in people, processes and/or systems,
    • Security risks, and/or issues
  • You must not tamper with various security controls implemented within the organization.
  • You must always comply with the non-disclosure agreements, even after employment with {Company_Name} has been terminated.

Acceptable Use of IT Assets and Resources

  • Company assets and resources should be used correctly and for its intended business purposes only. The correct usage means:
    • Within the legal, regulatory and compliance frameworks
    • In compliance with the information security policies, processes and procedures
    • As per the operating procedures or as intended by the manufacturer
  • You are responsible for the proper and safe keeping of company assets and resources assigned to you.
  • You must not alter configurations (hardware and software) in anyway without proper authorisation. This includes installing various software components downloaded from the Internet or otherwise.
  • {Company_Name} may grant privileges that are required to perform your role effectively and efficiently. Any privileges thus granted must not be misused.
  • All assets and resources must be returned promptly after its intended use is over or the employment has been terminated.
  • Assets and resources of {Company_Name} must be used only with due authentication and authorisation.
  • You must lock or close your workstation when not in use. Computers must not be left unattended at any time.

Protecting User Accounts and Digital Identity

  • {Company_Name} issues its staff, contractors and other associates with authentication credentials (including any passwords, PINs, codes, etc.) as part of accessing its assets and resources. These credentials must be kept secret and not be shared with anyone else.
  • These credentials must not be scribbled in places where others are able to see.
  • Any authentication token devices assigned to you must be kept securely where others have no access to it.

Using Your Own Device

  • {Company_Name} does not allow bringing your own devices to the workplace, except for mobile phones or tablets from which email access is granted.
  • The mobile devices used to access company information must be secured according to {Company_Name}’s prescribed configurations.
  • {Company_Name} must have the right to remote wipe (i.e., erase at operating system level) your mobile device under following situations if it is used for data storage:
    • The device is lost, or
    • If the employment contract and/or working agreement has been terminated

Your Right to Privacy

  • Your personal information must be kept private and protected in accordance with the personal data protection acts that are applicable to the jurisdiction of {Company_Name}. However, the location where it is stored, how it is protected, and/or processed will depend on the technology / solutions and services that the company will be using from time-to-time.
  • Currently or in the future, {Company_Name}’s facilities may have CCTV and other types of monitoring devices such as finger printing, card scanning, etc. Recordings from such devices are retained by the company for security, compliance and other business purposes.
  • {Company_Name} has implemented various security controls which captures logs, user behaviour and analytics information based on your interactions with information and/or information systems. Logs and alerts from such systems must be retained by as per {Company_Name} backup and data retention plans.

Usage of Internet and Email

  • Usage of internet and email must be for business purposes only. However, some limited personal use is permitted without causing an impact to personal productivity, and in compliance with {Company_Name}’s policies.
  • You must familiarise yourself with the proper emails and internet services usage etiquettes as mentioned in {Company_Name} security awareness course materials and act accordingly.

Intellectual Property Rights

  • You must always protect the intellectual property rights of {Company_Name}, by classifying the information appropriately and handling them in accordance with the data classification and handling policies.
  • You must not violate the intellectual property rights of {Company_Name} and of others.
  • Information obtained from the Internet must be checked before using them to prevent potential intellectual property violations.

Using Social Media

  • Unless authorised staff must not engage in social media interactions for the company.
  • Staff must not post company information or their own private opinions about the company in the social media.
  • Outside of work, staff may engage in social media for their own personal purposes, in a manner that causes no reputational damages to {Company_Name}.
