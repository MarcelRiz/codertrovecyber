Purpose of this Policy

The Purpose is to implement a security awareness and training program for all {Company_Name} company staff, contractors and relevant third parties.

{Company_Name} understands that “people”, not necessarily technology, are often the largest threat to security and sensitive information and will make every effort to ensure all company staff, contractors and relevant third parties are aware and adhere to the policy.

Policy Statements

All company staff, contractors and relevant third parties must be trained in and understand all {Company_Name} security policies and procedures. In addition, all staff and contractors are trained on how to identify, report, and prevent potential security incidents.

Security training is an on-going activity at {Company_Name}. Periodic security reminders keep Full time and Contract team members up to date with potential new threats. The frequency and form of these reminders vary and include things like security-related notices, emails, and verbal communication.

{Company_Name} has developed specific policies to identify core activities such as security reminders, protection, login monitoring, and password management. All staff and contractors are trained on these policies as part of their orientation.

{Company_Name} staff and contractors are responsible for understanding and following all security-related policies and procedures and asking their manager for clarification when needed. Company management is responsible for ensuring that staff and contractors are trained and up to date on the latest security threats.

{Company_Name} has also ensured that periodic reminders are in place of the policy’s existence and the latest version of the policy is kept for reference on our internal document-sharing site.
